The weekly US Ops meeting, 30 minutes

  1. 124 hrs beforeEveryone posts their check-in in the Tracker: what got done, what you're working on now, any roadblocks or risks. The lead reads them before the call
  2. 20-3 minOne win: a chapter or teammate shout-out, led by a different person each week
  3. 33-15 minOverdue and blocked: only the items flagged in the Tracker. The owner gives a new date or names what they need
  4. 415-25 minOne decision: a single topic picked in advance and framed as a question, led by whoever raised it
  5. 525-30 minCommitments: each person says what they own next week. The Tracker is updated live
  6. 6AfterRecap posted, with an owner and a date on every action item

How we track work

  • Every task on the grid has one owning role; the owner sets a date
  • Every action item from a meeting goes in the recap with an owner and a date
  • Open items roll into one tracker and get reviewed on the weekly call until closed
  • Owners mark items done or blocked; nothing closes silently
  • Priorities and tasks, like the current shared sheet but built into the app
  • A "US Ops HQ" tab in the app for the US Ops team: the to-do tracker, the task grid and the playbook in one place

Red zone and pause

Red zoneA chapter with under 30 signups, or any parent complaint in the past month. Its owner steps in with extra support right away: a call with the chapter leads and a plan to get back on track.
PauseA chapter that misses 2 key deadlines (choosing a location, setting the leadership team, selecting session dates), after being reminded 3+ times before each. Its owner meets with the chapter to push the season back, given the missed deadlines.

Other chapter rules

  • If a season is pushed back: pause the chapter in the app, update the web page, email signups (copy info@runyourcity.org)
  • Merge a chapter into a nearby one, or move a family, when that serves kids better
  • Flag struggling chapters and wins every week, so they get support or recognition
